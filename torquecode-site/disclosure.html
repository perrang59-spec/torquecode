Affiliate disclosure.

The short version: some links on TorqueCode are affiliate links. They do not cost you anything extra, and they do not change the diagnostic or safety advice you read here.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

What an affiliate link means

When you follow certain links from TorqueCode to a retailer, that retailer may pay us a small commission if you make a qualifying purchase. The price you pay is the same whether you use our link or go to the retailer directly. You are never charged extra, and we do not receive your payment details.

We only use affiliate links for products that are genuinely relevant to this site: diagnostic scan tools, code readers, multimeters, gauges, test kits, maintenance tools and fuel-saving accessories.

Our rules for recommendations

  • Commissions never influence a diagnosis, a test order, or a safety warning. If a test before replacement is the right answer, that is what we will say.
  • We do not accept payment for a specific product recommendation or for a favourable review.
  • We do not buy products through our own affiliate links.
  • We do not use link shorteners or hide where a link goes. You will always be able to see that a link leads to Amazon.
  • We do not copy prices, star ratings or customer reviews from Amazon into this site. Check current price, availability and full details on the retailer's own page.

Amazon and your privacy

When you click through to Amazon, Amazon may set cookies and collect information under its own privacy notice. We do not control those cookies and we do not receive your Amazon account or purchase information. See our Privacy Policy for how analytics and third-party links are handled on this site.
