Privacy policy.

TorqueCode has no accounts and no sign-up forms. Most of what the site remembers is stored in your own browser, on your own device. This page explains the analytics and third-party links that are the exceptions.

Information stored on your device

We use your browser's local storage to remember your preferences: theme choice, unit system (miles/gallons or kilometres/litres), saved trouble codes and your progress through the tips checklist. This information stays on your device. We do not send it to a server, and we do not use it to identify you.

You can clear it at any time by clearing site data for torquecode.co.za in your browser settings.

Analytics and cookies

We use Google Tag Manager to load Google Analytics 4, which helps us understand which pages are useful and how visitors move through the site. It may record page views, scroll events, approximate region, browser and device type, and referring page. We use this information in aggregate; we do not use it to make decisions about individuals.

Google may set cookies or similar identifiers for this purpose. You can block or delete cookies in your browser, use Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on, or adjust your Google Ads settings to limit personalized advertising. Disabling cookies does not break the core site â€” the code library, tips and calculator still work.

For details on how Google handles data, see the Google Privacy Policy.

Affiliate and external links

Some links on this site lead to Amazon or other third-party websites. When you click one, that third party may set its own cookies and collect information under its own privacy notice. We do not control those practices and we do not receive your account details, order history or payment information from them.

Our relationship with Amazon is described in the Affiliate Disclosure.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or ID.
  • We do not sell, rent or trade personal information.
  • We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
  • We do not show ads supplied by ad networks on this site.
