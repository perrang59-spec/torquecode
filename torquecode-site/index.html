The four families

P, B, C or U?

P

Powertrain

Engine, transmission, fuel delivery, ignition and emissions. The majority of check engine light codes.

B

Body

Airbags, climate control, lighting, locks and comfort systems. Often manufacturer-specific.

C

Chassis

ABS, traction control, steering, suspension and brake systems.

U

Network

CAN bus and module-to-module communication. Often caused by voltage or wiring faults.
